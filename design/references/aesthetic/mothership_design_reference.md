# The Brutalist Void: Visual Design Report of *Mothership RPG*
*An exhaustive analysis of Information Architecture, Color Theory, Typography, and Risograph Textures in Tuesday Knight Games' Sci-Fi Horror RPG.*

---

## Executive Summary

*Mothership* (created by **Sean McCoy** and published by **Tuesday Knight Games**) revolutionized tabletop RPG layout and graphic design. Winner of multiple ENNIE Awards, the game rejects traditional dense multi-column fantasy text blocks in favor of **Brutalist Information Architecture**, **Cassette Futurism**, and **Industrial Safety Manual Aesthetics**.

The design axioms of *Mothership* are:
1. **Control-Panel Spreads**: Every rule, mechanic, and ship procedure is self-contained within a 2-page spread—no page-flipping during life-and-death situations.
2. **Extreme Graphic Contrast**: Monochromatic black-and-white spreads sliced by searing industrial spot colors (Hazard Orange, Toxic Cyan).
3. **Zine Tactility & Bureaucratic Horror**: High-grain risograph printing, photocopied misregistration, flowchart decision trees, and OSHA-style hazard warnings.

## 1. Color Palette: The Dual-Spread Architecture & Spot Accents

*Mothership* does **not** use a uniform background throughout its books. Instead, Sean McCoy developed an intentional **dual-mode spread system** alternating between **Light Spreads** and **Inverted Dark Spreads** to physically pace the reader and signal escalating tension.

### A. The Page Background Reality: Neither Pure White nor Pure Black
1. **Light Spreads (The Operational State — Rules, Guides, Warden's Operations)**:
   * Direct pixel sampling from the official PDF (such as Page 14 *"Creating Compelling Answers"*) demonstrates that the background is **never pure `#FFFFFF`**.
   * It is a distinct, muted **Technical Cement / Cool Newsprint Grey** (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#EBEBEB;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#EBEBEB` / RGB 235, 235, 235), alternating in certain physical zine printings with a warmer **Industrial Manila / Newsprint** (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#EAE5D9;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#EAE5D9`).
   * The text, table headers, and top banners (*"14 CREATING COMPELLING ANSWERS"*) are likewise **not `#000000`**; they are rendered in a dense **Deep Charcoal Slate** (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#2E2A2C;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#2E2A2C` / RGB 46, 42, 44).
2. **Dark / Inverted Spreads (The "Panic / Crisis" State)**:
   * Used for the Panic Table, Stress rules, Mercenaries, The Void, and horror modules (*Dead Planet*, *Gradient Descent*).
   * Background: **Not pure `#000000`**. Instead, it uses an inky **Matte Carbon / Off-Black** (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#111215;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#111215`), allowing true black (`#000000`) ink lines, border frames, and illustrations to maintain distinct visual depth.

## 2. Typographic Identity

Typography in *Mothership* functions as an active game engine. Text size, weight, and hierarchy dictate the rhythm and urgency of reading.

```
   _____  .____   ________ .________  _______   
  /     \ |   |  /  _____/ |   ____/ /  _____/  
 /  \ /  \|   | /   \  ___ |____  \ /   \  ___  
/    Y    \   | \    \_\  \/       \\    \_\  \ 
\____|__  /___|  \______  /______  / \______  / 
        \/              \/       \/         \/  
   [   A  R  G  O  N    /    F  U  T  U  R  A   ]
```

### A. Display & Logo Typography
* **The Mothership Wordmark**: **Argon** (Argon 400 with custom geometric modifications).
  * A distinctive modern multi-line display typeface by Tom Anders Watkins. McCoy and Tuesday Knight Games linked specific letters (like the **"O"** and **"T"**) with custom geometric ligatures, creating an iconic retro-futuristic labyrinthine emblem.

### B. Structural Headings & Navigation
* **Primary Display & Chapter Titles**: **Futura LT Pro** (Bold, Heavy, and Condensed).
  * Used in all-caps, tightly tracked, frequently reversed out of solid black or fluorescent orange blocks.
* **Alternative Display Faces**: **Eurostile Bold Extended** and **Druk Wide**.
  * Used for ultra-wide, low-profile horizontal warning bands and equipment categories.

### C. Body Copy & Flowchart Hierarchies
* **Core Body Text**: **Futura** and **Helvetica / Univers**.
  * Unlike classical RPGs that use serif fonts (like Book Antiqua or Garamond), *Mothership* relies exclusively on clean, dense sans-serifs set with minimal leading to pack maximum utility into each spread.
* **Monospaced Data & Code Tables**: **Source Code Pro**, **Consolas**, and **OCR-A**.
  * Used for dice formula tags (`[1d10]`, `[STAT/2]`), weapon stats, patch tables, and terminal code logs.

## 3. Textures, Layout Architecture & Physicality

*Mothership* treats the physical book (or digital PDF spread) as a tactical instrument rather than a narrative book.

```
[ BRUTALIST ZINE PRINT ]                   [ INDUSTRIAL DIEGETIC UI ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Uncoated Rough Recycled Paper          ⟷    Black Box Reversal Banners
Risograph Halftone Ink Bleed           ⟷    Bracketed Fillable Checkboxes [ ]
Offset Color Misregistration           ⟷    Decision-Tree Flowchart Connectors
High-Contrast Xerox Grain              ⟷    Vector Isometric Hull Schematics
Folded Staple-Bound Zine Spine         ⟷    Heavy Structural Border Grids
```

### A. Layout Tropes & Information Architecture
1. **Flowcharts & Decision Trees**:
   * Character creation and critical combat resolutions are presented as interconnected flowchart bubbles connected by directional arrows, enabling a complete character roll in under 5 minutes.
2. **Form Elements & Bracket Notation**:
   * Interactive elements are coded with computer bracket syntax: `[ ] SAVE VS FEAR`, `[x] AMMO DELETED`, `[2d10 DMG]`.
3. **Visual Chunking**:
   * Information is bounded by 1pt to 3pt black outline boxes, mimicking the control consoles of industrial drilling rigs or space tugs.

### B. Materiality & Print Degradation
* **Risograph Imperfections**: Deliberate stippled halftone grain, coarse ink coverage, and subtle horizontal registration shifts between the black ink plate and neon spot colors.
* **Photocopied High-Contrast Imagery**: Illustrations feature deep inky shadows, stark silhouettes, and xerox-style stippling that evokes found security footage and bootlegged survival manuals.

## 4. Geometric Forms, Silhouettes & Spatial Anatomy

*Mothership* is constructed entirely on **Modular Technical Schematics, Blueprint Grids, and Diagnostic Flowcharts**:

### A. The Structural Foundation: Crisp Rectangular Grids & Dossier Boxes
* **1pt to 3pt Black Outline Boxes**: Every section of information (weapons, skills, stats, inventory) is quarantined inside a hard rectangular cell. There are no floating, unanchored text blocks.
* **Full-Spread Header Bands**: Long, solid rectangular black banners spanning the full width of the spread containing inverted white sans-serif headings.

### B. Flowchart Nodes & Tracking Circles
* **Circular Step Nodes**: Character creation decision-trees utilize circular numbered nodes (`①`, `②`, `③`) and circular flowchart bubbles that branch into sub-tables.
* **Circular Stress & Panic Pips**: Unfilled circular tracking bubbles (`○ ○ ○ ○ ●`) for tracking accumulating Stress and Panic checks.
* **Radial Dice Indicators**: Circular target-lock dials and radial percentage wheels representing life-support and oxygen depletion.

### C. Stadium Pills & Barcode Strips
* **Pill-Shaped Status Badges**: Rounded stadium badges (`( STATUS: OFFLINE )`) used for system warnings, clearance ranks, and armor type callouts.
* **Barcode & Calibration Tapes**: Alternating black-and-white vertical stripe rectangles mimicking industrial barcodes and optical measurement test-patterns.

### D. Hexagons & Isometric Grids
* **Hex-Crawl Sector Maps**: Space sectors, navigation charts, and derelict ship drift patterns are mapped onto strict hexagonal coordinate grids.
* **Isometric Hull Cutaways**: Ship blueprints are presented as 30° isometric wireframes showing modular rectangular cargo bays and cylindrical drive cores.

## 5. Synthesis: Design Rules for *Mothership*-Inspired Projects

1. **Color Rule**: Adhere to strict two-tone austerity: stark black (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#0F1115;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#0F1115`) and clean white/manila (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#F4F5F7;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#F4F5F7`). Introduce a single high-voltage spot color—such as Hazard Orange (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#FF5500;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#FF5500`) or Terminal Cyan (<span style="display:inline-block;width:12px;height:12px;background:#00E5FF;border:1px solid #777;border-radius:2px;vertical-align:middle;margin-right:4px;"></span>`#00E5FF`)—strictly for actionable game-critical data.
2. **Typography Rule**: Use **Argon** or wide geometric sans for primary titles. Use **Futura** for body and subheadings. Use **OCR-A** or monospace for rules and dice brackets.
3. **Form & Geometry Rule**: Structure information in **dense rectangular control boxes** linked by **circular flowchart nodes and stadium pill badges**. Never float content without crisp structural boundaries.
4. **Layout Rule**: Zero page flips for a single procedure. Design in complete, isolated two-page spreads using thick gridlines, flowcharts, and bracketed checkboxes.
