# Codex Presentation & UX Report: *Mythic Bastionland*
*An exhaustive analysis of Information Architecture, Arthurian Illuminated Borders, Strict 2-Page Oddpoch Spreads, and Minimalist Micro-Table Layout in Chris McDowall's Chivalric RPG.*

---

## Executive Summary

*Mythic Bastionland: Before Into the Odd* (written and designed by **Chris McDowall**, illustrated by **Alec Sorensen**, and published by **Bastionland Press**) represents the absolute zenith of modern minimalist tabletop design. Released in 2024/2025 following a historic crowdfunding campaign, the game reimagines chivalric Arthurian myth and feudal folk-horror through the lens of McDowall's signature design school.

Where other RPGs fill 400 pages with dense world-building and cross-referencing sub-systems, *Mythic Bastionland* accomplishes equal or greater mechanical and narrative depth in 212 pages through an unyielding commitment to **The 2-Page Self-Contained Unit ("The Oddpoch")**, **Embedded Micro-Tables**, and **Illuminated Modernist Layout**.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE CORE DESIGN AXIOMS OF MYTHIC BASTIONLAND              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. The Oddpoch (Strict 2-Page Spreads): Every Knight, Myth, Holding,    │
│    and procedure begins and ends on a single open two-page spread.      │
│    A page turn never separates a rule from its context.                 │
│ 2. Modernized Illuminated Manuscripts: Classic Arthurian heraldic       │
│    foliage vines and tapestry banners frame razor-sharp dual-column     │
│    modernist typography.                                                │
│ 3. Micro-Table Decision Engines: Rules do not explain theory; they      │
```

## 1. Information Architecture & Macro-Organization

*Mythic Bastionland* is structured with surgical precision. The entire rulebook is organized as a modular atlas of chivalric archetypes and mythical hazards:

```
[ FRONT MATTER ] ──> P.1-12:   Chivalric Verse, Primer & Core Rules
[ CORE ENGINE  ] ──> P.13-26:  Holdings, Authority, Warbands & Seasons
[ THE KNIGHTS  ] ──> P.27-130: 52 Distinct Knightly Orders (Each Exactly 2 Pages)
[ THE MYTHS    ] ──> P.131-190: 30 Primeval Myths & Omens (Each Exactly 2 Pages)
[ REFEREE HUB  ] ──> P.191-212: Exploration Engine, Sparks & Encounter Matrix
```

### A. The 52 Knights (P.27-130)
The heart of the book contains 52 individual Knightly Orders (e.g., *The Moss Knight*, *The Falcon Knight*, *The Brier Knight*, *The Gilded Knight*). Each Knight occupies **exactly one 2-page spread**:
- **Left Page**: Evocative background verse, Knightly Oath, Passions, and full-page atmospheric illustration.
- **Right Page**: Starting Vigor/Clarity, Steed, Relic or Peculiar Weapon, and three rollable tables (e.g., *Childhood Spark*, *The Knightly Shame*, *The Heraldic Coat*).

### B. The 30 Myths (P.131-190)
Like the Knights, every Myth (the towering, primeval anomalies and supernatural threats that plague the Realm) is completely self-contained within its own 2-page spread:
- Left page: The Omens (what players hear and see before the creature arrives) and the Beast's nature.
- Right page: The Myth's stats, combat reactions, and the Boon awarded if bound or slain.

### A. The Triple-Layer Decorative Framing
*Mythic Bastionland* uses three distinct visual anchors on every page:
1. **The Top Tapestry Runner**: A full-bleed, horizontal medieval border running along the top edge, featuring intertwined forest green vines, gold leaves, and heraldic creatures on dark teal cloth.
2. **The Left/Right Vine Flutes**: A delicate, pencil-thin illuminated manuscript border of Celtic/Arthurian knotwork and climbing vines running down the outer margin.
3. **The Heraldic Coin Folio**: The page number is housed inside an antique pewter-and-bronze circular medallion at the bottom outer corner, stamped with an engraved knight holding an upright longsword.

### B. The Symmetrical Two-Column Matrix
- Content is arranged in two crisp columns per page.
- Text never meanders. Headings are set in **Small Caps** with wide tracking, immediately followed by 1 or 2 tight explanatory sentences.
- When an illustrative plate appears, it snaps precisely into one of the vertical column boundaries, maintaining strict structural alignment.

## 3. Typographic Identity & Monastic-Modernist Voice

The typographic personality of *Mythic Bastionland* balances the gravitas of a **15th-century Gutenberg Bible** with the razor-sharp legibility of **Swiss modernist typography**.

### A. Typography Breakdown

| Role | Typeface Category | Style / Weight | Usage & Visual Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Macro Headers & Titles** | **Grand Gothic Blackletter / Old English**| High-Contrast Medieval Calligraphy | Page titles (`Authority`, `The Knight of the Thorn`), cover branding, opening chivalric poetry. |
| **Section Headings** | **Classical Roman Serif (Caslon / Adobe Garamond)** | Small Caps, Bold, Tracked +80 | `MUSTERING TROOPS`, `TAXES, TITHES, & LEVIES`, `COURTLY CONFLICT`, `CONQUEST`. |
| **Body Copy** | **Garamond / Plantin** | Regular, 9.5pt / 13pt leading | Clean, warm, high-legibility book serif with balanced proportions. |
| **Table Headers & Metadata** | **ITC Franklin Gothic / Modern Sans** | Bold Small Caps, Inverted White on Charcoal | Table titles (`INCREASED COLLECTIONS`, `DRAMA IN COURT`), stat abbreviations (`VIGOR`, `CLARITY`). |
| **Table Dice & Numerals** | **Monospaced / Tabular Sans** | Bold, 10pt | Dice ranges (`1`, `2-3`, `4-6`, `d12`, `d20`), stat numbers. |

### B. The Tone of Chivalric Economy
McDowall writes with an unyielding poetic economy. Consider the rule for **Conquest** on P.21:
> *"Knights live between two worlds. One of rank, oath, and rule of the worthy. The other of chaos, brutality, and rule of the strong.*  
> *Often, having the audacity to seat yourself in a Holding is enough to make you the ruler.*  
> *Holdings and Realms seized by force and left unchallenged tend to have a period of turmoil before adapting to the new status quo."*

Three sentences give the Referee complete philosophical, political, and mechanical guidance.

## 5. Micro-Tables as Immediate Decision Engines

The defining mechanical presentation feature of *Mythic Bastionland* is the **Embedded Micro-Table**. Where other games write paragraphs explaining penalties and edge cases, *Mythic Bastionland* uses compact 3-tier or 6-tier tables embedded directly under the heading:

### Example: Increased Collections (P.21)
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 INCREASED COLLECTIONS                  │
├───────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│   1   │ Immediate misrule.                             │
├───────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  2-3  │ Your coffers are filled, but take a Crisis.    │
├───────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  4-6  │ The Vassals pay willingly, mostly.             │
└───────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Zero Ambiguity**: The Referee and player roll a d6; the outcome is immediately tangible.
- **Narrative Hooks Built into Rules**: Even a success (*"mostly"*) or partial failure (*"take a Crisis"*) immediately propels the fiction forward.
- **High-Contrast Dark Headers**: Every table header is an inverted dark charcoal bar with crisp white bold lettering, creating instant visual anchors that pull the eye directly to the actionable rule.

## 6. Key Takeaways & Actionable Codex UX Strategies

1. **The Strict Zero-Page-Turn Rule ("The Unit View")**:
   - In digital codex design, every archetype (class, faction, monster, ship) should fit completely within a single viewport or single non-scrolling card. When users must jump between pages or tabs to read one unit, mental bandwidth is squandered.
2. **Illuminated Framing for Diegetic Immersion**:
   - Subtle decorative borders (heraldic vines, tapestry runners, or terminal telemetry frames in sci-fi) instantly signal genre and prestige without sacrificing clean typographical readability.
3. **Micro-Tables Over Explanatory Paragraphs**:
   - Whenever an action has uncertain consequences, condense the resolution into a high-contrast 3-row or 6-row outcome table instead of multi-paragraph rule descriptions.
4. **Muted Organic Wash as Base Canvas**:
   - Softening the digital background to an organic tone (such as Sage Wash `#E6EAE4` or Deep Carbon `#101214`) prevents eye fatigue and makes high-contrast table headers pop dramatically.
